# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Planet and moon coordinates show only their link, without the copy and menu
  buttons beside them. Right-click a coordinate, or long-press it on touch, to
  copy it, share a location link or prepare a mission.

- The command orb can announce selected new features in game. Each announcement
  shows once and links to its full update.

- Intergalactic Research Network links eligible labs across your planets to shorten
  research time. Research details show contributing planets and their lab levels.
  Read the [build note](docs/news/changes/2026-11-06-research-network.md).

- Deployment shows an estimated arrival date and time instead of a duplicate
  duration. Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744)
  and [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for independently
  requesting it. Read the [build note](docs/news/changes/2026-11-05-deployment-arrival.md).

- Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for the
  verified report of frequent connection warnings, and Ethan Mick for confirming
  it. Read the [connection recovery build note](docs/news/changes/2026-11-05-connection-recovery.md).

- Combat Simulator can load your selected planet or moon as defender and show
  sampled debris-field averages and ranges. Thanks to
  [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for the
  [owned-world import request](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1567803737630703627).
  Read the [build note](docs/news/changes/2026-11-05-simulator-imports-and-debris.md).

- Thanks to Brian Mick ([@bmick](https://github.com/bmick)), our first external
  contributor, for the foundation and original implementation of the layered
  insignia composer ([#302](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/pull/302)).
  Read the [build note](docs/news/changes/2026-11-02-layered-insignia-composer.md).

- Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
  reporting confusing manufacturing timers. Read the
  [single-order timer build note](docs/news/changes/2026-11-05-manufacturing-timers.md).
