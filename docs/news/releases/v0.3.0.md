Orbit Imperium 0.3.0 adds Heavy Fighters and Cruisers, Laser and Ion Technology,
and Light Laser and Ion Cannon defenses. There are more choices in what you
research, what you build, and what you send into a fight.

We've also spent time on the parts of the game you use every day. You can name
your planets, move from coordinates straight to the galaxy, and see more useful
information before committing resources or launching a fleet.

## More ships, research, and defenses

- **Heavy Fighters** require Shipyard 3, Armour Technology 2, and Impulse Drive 2.
  They have rapid fire against Small Cargo ships and Espionage Probes.
- **Laser Technology** requires Research Lab 1 and Energy Technology 2. Reach
  Laser Technology 3 with Shipyard 2 to build **Light Lasers**.
- **Ion Technology** requires Research Lab 4, Energy Technology 4, and Laser
  Technology 5. It opens the path to Cruisers and Ion Cannons.
- **Cruisers** require Shipyard 5, Ion Technology 2, and Impulse Drive 4. Their
  rapid fire is effective against Light Fighters, Rocket Launchers, and probes.
- **Ion Cannons** require Shipyard 4 and Ion Technology 4, adding a defense with
  stronger shields to your options.

New content requires explicit universe activation after deployment. The official
server's deployment and activation are being coordinated with this release;
self-hosted universes follow their operator's rollout.

## Less work to get around

Building, research, shipyard, and defense details share a clearer layout with
costs, requirements, benefits, and active work close to the action controls.
Orders confirm beside the button you used. Building and research links keep the
selected planet, and resource production settings are available from the menu.

Galaxy views bring mission actions closer to their targets. Coordinate links open
the right galaxy location, fleet dispatch previews arrival and return times, and
fleet details load before switching planets. Compact controls and fleet rows fit
smaller screens more reliably.

Reports have more useful previews and simpler detail screens. Opening a report
marks it read. You can delete reports from your own list and restore them later;
other players' copies stay intact. Espionage buildings follow the same order as
the catalog, and probe-only espionage returns no longer add redundant reports.

## Planets, players, and sessions

You can rename your planets, and those names appear throughout the game. Public
supporter names and cosmetics can appear alongside player identities. These are
cosmetic; they add no gameplay advantage.

Active play renews the sign-in session, with a 30-day inactivity window. Background
updates alone don't keep a session alive, and signing out still ends it. Optional
analytics failures no longer interrupt the game. The homepage now includes a
direct link to the Discord community.

## Upgrading a self-hosted universe

Back up your database and retain your previous image and active rules. Install
`orbit-imperium:0.3.0` across every API, worker, and operator process before
activating content. Startup adds migration 15 for optional planet names. Existing
unnamed planets retain their fallback labels.

For Accounts-backed installations, deploy the companion Accounts support for
`/api/auth/game-session/renew` before this Orbit version. The
[rolling-session guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.3.0/docs/operations/rolling-sessions.md)
explains renewal and compatibility. Local authentication also supports the new
inactivity window.

Deployment preserves saved universe rules. Start from a live export, retain its
universe ID and base revision, and use the supplied preparation scripts for
content you haven't enabled yet. Enable Impulse Drive before Heavy Fighters;
Laser Technology before Ion Technology; and Ion Technology before Cruisers or
Ion Cannons. The Light Laser script enables Laser Technology and Light Lasers
together. Review `orbit rules diff`, apply with the deployed binary, then export
again and run `orbit audit`. See the
[rules operations guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.3.0/docs/operations/rules-updates.md),
[Heavy Fighter guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.3.0/docs/operations/heavy-fighter.md),
[Laser guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.3.0/docs/operations/light-laser.md),
and [Ion guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.3.0/docs/operations/ion-technology.md).
The additional scripts are `scripts/prepare-cruiser-rules.mjs` and
`scripts/prepare-ion-cannon-rules.mjs`.

Activation retains existing progress and accepted work. Refresh clients after
activation. Keep definitions needed by owned units and paid jobs; don't downgrade
to a binary that cannot read the active content. Beginner protection's multiplier
is now configurable per universe; existing rules retain the default multiplier
of five unless an operator explicitly changes it.

The game no longer bundles `/source.tar.gz` or its download link. Source remains
available from the repository at the release tag, including GitHub's source
archives. The release provides a Linux amd64 Docker archive and `SHA256SUMS`.
License and dependency notices remain in the image.

See the [GitHub release](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/releases/tag/v0.3.0)
for downloads and the
[full source comparison](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/compare/v0.2.0...v0.3.0)
for all changes.

Moons, expeditions, and colony abandonment remain unavailable. The official beta
may reset before full public launch; this update does not reset the universe.
