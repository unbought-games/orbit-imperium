Orbit Imperium 0.2.0 adds the research and ships you need to expand beyond your
empire. Research Impulse Drive and Astrophysics, build a Colony Ship, and send
it out into the galaxy to establish another planet.

There's also a new Empire overview, more useful combat reports, and a collection
of fixes to make the game easier to navigate. Thank you for all the feedback!

## Expand your empire

- **Impulse Drive** becomes available with Research Lab 2 and Energy Technology 1.
  At level 5, Small Cargo ships switch to impulse propulsion, with a higher
  base speed and fuel coefficient. Fleets already in flight keep their accepted
  travel times and fuel costs.
- **Astrophysics** requires Research Lab 3, Espionage Technology 4, and Impulse
  Drive 3. Research levels determine how many planets you can own and which
  positions you can colonize.
- **Colony Ships** require Shipyard 4 and Impulse Drive 3. A successful
  colonization consumes one ship and establishes a planet with its own size,
  temperature, and room to grow. Cargo arrives with the colony; extra ships
  and escorts return. If colonization fails, the fleet returns with its ships
  and cargo.
- **Deployment** lets you move ships and cargo between your own planets. Galaxy
  actions and fleet dispatch explain research requirements and colony capacity.

These features require universe rules activation after installation. The official
server's deployment and activation are being coordinated with this release;
self-hosted universes follow their operator's rollout.

## A clearer view of the game

The new **Empire** overview brings your planets, production, queues, and fleet
activity together. Navigation stays within the planet or task you're working on,
with more compact headers and pinned navigation on desktop and mobile.

Combat reports make losses, survivors, loot, and debris easier to read. New
reports attribute firing and damage by side and origin for each round. Unread
reports now show up in navigation, and galaxy views and reports display public
player names.

## Fixes and everyday improvements

- Production percentages save automatically when changed.
- Temporary connection failures recover automatically, including transient
  failures while communicating with Accounts.
- Fleet dispatch validates destinations and explains why a launch is blocked.
- Galaxy views show beginner protection and disable attacks against protected
  targets.
- Locked buildings remain inspectable, and building actions refresh correctly
  after construction or cancellation.
- Resource and energy quotations use clearer numbers. Zero-capacity storage no
  longer produces a misleading full-storage warning.
- Universes have names and readable URLs. Installed home-screen navigation stays
  inside the app.

## Upgrading a self-hosted universe

Back up your database and retain the previous image and active rules before
upgrading. Install `orbit-imperium:0.2.0` across every API, worker, and operator
process before enabling new content. Startup applies the included schema
migrations for enrollment timestamps, universe names, analytics, and colonization.
Existing enrollment timestamps remain unknown where they were not recorded.

Deployment preserves your saved rules. Export them and apply the supplied
preparation scripts in order: Impulse Drive, Astrophysics, then colonization.
Use a fresh export and review `orbit rules diff` before each `orbit rules apply`.
The [colonization rollout guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.2.0/docs/operations/colonization.md)
and [rules operations guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/v0.2.0/docs/operations/rules-updates.md)
cover activation, verification, and compatibility. Keep your own universe ID and
settings; the official beta's identifiers in the rollout guide are not defaults
for independent servers.

Activation preserves existing progress and accepted work. Refresh clients to load
the new catalog. Once new rules or state are in use, don't downgrade to a binary
that cannot read them; disabling new launches doesn't undo accepted flights or
remove colonies.

Download the source archive, Linux amd64 Docker archive, and `SHA256SUMS` from the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/releases/tag/v0.2.0).
See the [full source comparison](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/compare/v0.1.0...v0.2.0)
for all changes.
