id: develop-mine-energy
title: See mine energy use before upgrading
date: '2026-11-23'
---

The Resources list shows how much extra energy the next mine level will use at
your saved production settings. Look for the lightning bolt beside the upgrade
price. Desktop players can also see upgrade prices for power plants and storage
buildings in the list.
