title: Return to your prepared fleet
date: '2026-11-18'
---

Browser Back now returns to a prepared fleet after a visit to Briefing or another game screen. Dispatch checks the fleet and destination again before you can send it. Starting another dispatch or reloading the tab clears the previous review.

Thanks to Churak, Eleven, and Gh0stP1rate for reporting and investigating the lost fleet selections.
