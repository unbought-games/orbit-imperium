id: empire-identity
title: Give your empire a name
date: '2026-11-05T18:30:00Z'
---

We've added an identity editor to empire profiles. Give your empire a name, add a
short tagline, and choose the title the game uses to address you. Names and
text are free for every player, along with twelve starting titles.

Each empire can choose its own title. Profiles, dossiers, and the command orb
use that choice. If supporter access expires or a server removes a catalog,
your selection stays saved while the game uses Commander.

Self-hosters can install their own title catalogs without an Unbought account
service. Supporter collections are supplied separately for official servers;
achievement-specific titles will use the new permanent-award foundation.

This change is for source builds after 0.6.0. Availability depends on your
server's deployment and installed catalog.
