id: fleet-cargo-at-departure
title: Load maximum fleet cargo at departure
date: '2026-11-18'
---

**Add all** and the individual **Max** controls now load the whole resources
available at departure, after reserving flight fuel. Amounts you enter by hand
stay exact.

Fleet Review refreshes when you return to the tab and every 15 minutes while
it is open. If any cargo amount rises or falls by 10% or more, you can confirm
the new amounts on Review or go back to edit the dispatch.
