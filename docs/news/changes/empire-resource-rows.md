title: Exact resource balances on the Empire overview
date: '2026-11-18'
---

The Empire overview shows metal, crystal, and deuterium side by side when all three fit.
Narrower panels give each resource its own row.
You can read the full balance with its breakdown across worlds and fleets.
Each planet shows its storage meter, capacity, and hourly production with its balance.
