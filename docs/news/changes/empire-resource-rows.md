title: Exact resource balances on the Empire overview
date: '2026-11-18'
---

Metal, crystal, and deuterium each have a fixed row on the Empire overview.
You can read the full balance alongside resources on worlds and in flight.
Each planet shows its storage meter, capacity, and hourly production beside its balance.

[reporting the split digits on mobile](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1570888321868238918).
