title: Building fields on Develop
date: '2026-11-18'
---

Resources and Facilities now show how many fields you’ve used on the selected
planet or moon. When construction reserves a field, you’ll see that reservation
beside the count.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[suggesting this while planning construction on a colony](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1572477097790668921).
