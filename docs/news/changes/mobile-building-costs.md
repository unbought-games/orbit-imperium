title: Upgrade prices on mobile building lists
date: '2026-11-18'
---

On phones, Resources and Facilities show each building’s level beside its name
and the next upgrade’s price underneath. Large prices round up so you can plan
a shipment at a glance. Open the building for exact amounts.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[suggesting prices on the building list](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1572618259365367940).
