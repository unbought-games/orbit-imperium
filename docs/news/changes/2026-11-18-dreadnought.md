date: '2026-11-18'
---

Graviton Technology now unlocks the Dreadnought. You’ll need Shipyard 12,
Hyperspace Technology 6 and Hyperspace Drive 7 to build one. A planet or moon
with the right yard can take the order.

A Dreadnought carries a million resources and can tear through fighters,
satellites and light defenses with rapid fire. It’s also the slowest ship in
your fleet. You can try the matchup in the combat simulator before committing
five million metal, four million crystal and a million deuterium to the build.
