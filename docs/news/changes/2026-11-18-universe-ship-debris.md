title: Ship debris in Universe settings
date: '2026-11-18'
---

Under Settings → Help & community, Universe settings now shows how much of a
destroyed ship’s build cost becomes debris and which resources count. It also
explains how carried cargo, fuel, and debris affect moon formation.

Thanks to [eleven](https://discord.com/users/184060817644322816) for
[asking what happens to a destroyed ship’s cargo](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1571268656506208330).
