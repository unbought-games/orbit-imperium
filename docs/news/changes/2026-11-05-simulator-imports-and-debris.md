id: simulator-imports-and-debris
title: Practice against your defenses and see the debris
date: '2026-11-05T14:25:00Z'
---

Combat Simulator can now load your selected planet or moon as the defender.
It brings in your research and defenses, with a separate option to include
stationed ships. Importing replaces the defender inputs; your attacker and allied
fleets stay as you entered them. You can edit the imported values, and switching
worlds won't replace your draft.

Results now show the average and range of newly generated debris across the ten
sampled battles, using your universe's combat rules. That's debris created by
the battle, before any collection. The samples are estimates based on the forces
and assumptions you entered.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[requesting the owned-world import](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1567803737630703627).

These changes are included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your
server depends on its deployment.
