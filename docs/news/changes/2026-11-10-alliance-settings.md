# Clearer alliance settings

Alliance settings now have separate Profile, Recruitment, Invitations, and
Leadership sections. Names and tags sit together, related preferences share a
row on desktop, and dividing lines separate each task. Each form has its own
save action, and drafts survive switching sections.

Leadership transfer now uses one member selector and a confirmation dialog.
Invitation sharing, image downloads, and link controls have their own home,
without a large poster pushing the controls down the page.
