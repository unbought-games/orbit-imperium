---
id: jump-gates
title: Jump Gates connect your moons
date: '2026-11-13'
---

Build Jump Gates on two of your moons to move stationed ships between them
instantly. Choose a destination and ships from the gate's facility page, review
the transfer, then jump. Resources stay behind, and the transfer uses no fuel or
fleet slot.

Both gates cool down after a jump: one hour in a speed-1 universe, scaled by
universe speed. Gate upgrades do not shorten the cooldown. Construction requires
Lunar Base 1 and Hyperspace Technology 7.
