id: galaxy-probe-count
title: Remember your Galaxy probe count
date: '2026-11-10T23:10:00-05:00'
---

You can choose how many espionage probes Galaxy’s Spy action sends in Settings →
Galaxy espionage. The count defaults to one and is saved for your empire in each
universe, across planets, moons, and devices. Any positive whole number can be
saved, even if you don’t currently have that many probes.

Spy stays unavailable when the selected world has fewer probes, so it never
silently sends a smaller fleet.

Requested in [#110](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/issues/110).
