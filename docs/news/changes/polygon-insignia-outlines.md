title: Outlines for diamond and hexagon insignias
date: '2026-11-23'
---

Diamond and hexagon insignias now have an outline that follows each shape, so
dark designs are easier to see in the editor and beside player names.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for
[reporting the missing outlines](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1572963060593852488).
