id: deployment-arrival
title: Arrival times for deployments
date: '2026-11-05T14:00:00Z'
---

Deployment now shows an estimated arrival date and time alongside the travel
duration. It replaces the duplicate total flight duration, so you can see when
your ships will reach a planet or moon without opening a tooltip.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) and
[sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for independently
[requesting](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1567284728816730185)
[this improvement](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1567655649561673798).

This change is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your server
depends on its deployment.
