id: research-network
title: Link your laboratories with the Intergalactic Research Network
date: '2026-11-06T14:45:55Z'
---

The Intergalactic Research Network lets your planets work together on research.
Each completed level links one more eligible laboratory, shortening research time
without adding another research slot. Your starting planet still pays the full
cost, and every contributing lab must meet the technology's lab requirement.

Research details now show which planets contribute and their lab levels. Once a
project starts, its labs and completion time stay fixed. You can inspect the same
project from any planet.

This addition is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Existing servers need the
supporting deployment and standard content upgrade before it becomes available.
Self-hosters can follow the [activation guide](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/blob/main/docs/operations/intergalactic-research-network.md).
