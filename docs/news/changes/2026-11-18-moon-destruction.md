---
id: moon-destruction
title: Moon destruction missions
date: '2026-11-18'
---

You can send Dreadnoughts to destroy an enemy moon. They have to win the battle
first. Dispatch shows the chance of destroying the moon and the separate chance
of losing every ship you send. Both can happen on the same trip.

If you lose a moon, fleets already in space still have a way home. They return
to the parent planet on schedule. Incoming deliveries, deployments and allied
defenders head there too. An enemy attack already on the way reaches the empty
moon and turns back.
