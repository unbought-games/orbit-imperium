---
id: compact-notifications
title: Notifications take up less room
date: '2026-11-09T15:30:00Z'
---

Game notifications now stack behind the newest card, leaving more of Galaxy
visible while you send probes down the list. Swipe the top card away to reveal
the next notice, or open it for the full details.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) and
[sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for reporting
[obstructed Galaxy controls](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1568711994855653528)
and [mobile notifications](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1568712555927576687).

This change is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your server
depends on its deployment.
