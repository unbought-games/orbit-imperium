date: '2026-11-24'

The Combat Simulator shows attacker wins, defender wins, draws, and an average
round count. Cargo, haul, and debris have separate summaries. Each fleet's
setup shows ship and defense totals, with remaining units beside the inputs
after simulation.

Raid estimates use the same five-pass rule. The simulator shows how much
extra space the tightest winning sample needs for a full haul. Dispatch
shows available space after fuel and loaded cargo beside its estimated haul.
