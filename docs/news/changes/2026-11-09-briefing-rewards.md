---
id: briefing-rewards
title: Clearer briefing rewards
date: '2026-11-09T15:20:00Z'
---

Briefing resource amounts now have a visible **Reward** label. When you need more
resources for an upgrade, the briefing names the level that upgrade will reach.
An older unfinished directive can no longer make the cost of your next Solar
Plant upgrade look like the cost of level 1.

We've also removed the advisor's “Click me to hide this” hint. Clicking the orb
still hides the bubble.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for
[reporting the confusing amounts](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1568320712832450641),
and [eleven](https://discord.com/users/184060817644322816) for
[pointing out the misleading hint](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1568727933126709462).

This change is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your server
depends on its deployment.
