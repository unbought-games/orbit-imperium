date: '2026-11-28'

Each moon now appears indented beneath its planet in the body picker. Screen
readers announce the parent planet alongside the moon’s name and coordinates.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1575225211463147661)
for suggesting this and showing how two worlds at the same coordinates looked in the picker.
