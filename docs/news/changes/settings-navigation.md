title: Focused settings pages
date: '2026-11-12'
---

Settings now has separate pages for empire identity, game settings, rewards,
help, account details, and empire deletion. On desktop, the sidebar switches to
these sections. On mobile, Settings opens a menu, and each page has a Back to
Settings link.

You can edit your empire name, tagline, title, and insignia together. Vacation
mode lives under Game settings, and universe information is under Help &
community.
