date: '2026-11-02T18:09:52Z'
---

You can now combine a center and an accent to make an empire insignia in Settings.
Choose a background shape, adjust colors, or shuffle a coordinated palette.

Thanks to Brian Mick ([@bmick](https://github.com/bmick)), our first external
contributor, for the foundation and original implementation of the layered
insignia composer in [#302](https://github.com/unbought-games/orbit-imperium/pull/302).
Ethan's follow-up work integrated the composer, fixed compatibility and editing
issues, and added background choices.

This change is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your server
depends on its deployment and installed catalog.
