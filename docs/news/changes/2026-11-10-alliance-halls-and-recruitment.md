---

Alliances get a Hall with their own crest, artwork, motto, welcome message, and
noticeboard. We want it to feel like a place your members built together, while
keeping the management pages compact and practical.

All alliance customization is free, including image uploads and animated GIFs
and WebPs. Leaders can delegate editing through alliance roles, and the identity
stays with the alliance when leadership changes.

Public recruitment pages have three layouts: Flagship, Transmission, and Citadel.
Write your story in Markdown, link your community spaces, and preview the whole
page before saving. Each template keeps its own content when you switch layouts.

New commanders arriving through a recruitment link can join or apply during their
opening conversation with the orb, just after naming their world. Applications
still need approval, and players can begin their briefing while they wait.
