title: Direct links to alliance pages
date: '2026-11-13T00:00:00-05:00'
---

Every alliance section now has its own address, including each settings page.
You can bookmark or share a page, refresh it, and use your browser's Back and
Forward buttons without losing your place. Drafts stay with you as you move
between alliance sections. Selecting an alliance in the directory also updates
the address so you can return to its recruitment page directly.
