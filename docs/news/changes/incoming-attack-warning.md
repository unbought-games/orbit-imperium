title: A clearer incoming attack warning
date: '2026-11-28'
---

An incoming attack now puts a shield and the words **Incoming attack** at the top of the screen. Select **View fleets** to inspect the approach. The warning stays visible with reduced motion and clears when the attack is over. Espionage probes keep their quieter signal.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) and [Churak](https://discord.com/users/162718073336365056) for explaining how easily the red orb could be missed during an attack.
