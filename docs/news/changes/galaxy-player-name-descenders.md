title: Clearer player names in Galaxy
date: '2026-11-28'
---

Player names in Galaxy rows now show the full tails of letters like **p** and
**j** on small screens. Long names still end in an ellipsis.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for
[reporting the clipped letters](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1574579066877771806).
