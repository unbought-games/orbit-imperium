You can build planets, research technologies, and command fleets in Orbit Imperium.
There's more to build, and plenty to fix. You can't buy your way ahead.

This is an early beta. The game is incomplete, and the official universe may be
reset before the full public launch.

## What's included

- Planetary economies, construction, research, shipbuilding, and defenses.
- Fleets, combat, espionage, reports, and alliances.
- Standalone accounts for self-hosted universes, with optional OpenID Connect.
- Source downloads from the running game.

## Recent fixes

Game quantities display whole numbers rounded down, so the interface doesn't
promise resources you don't have. Navigation also keeps installed iOS web apps
inside the app.

## Running your own universe

Follow the repository's self-hosting instructions. These notes describe this
build; the deployment date and available version depend on the server operator.
