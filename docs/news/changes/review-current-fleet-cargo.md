title: Review current cargo before launch
date: '2026-11-28'
---

When you use **Add all** or **Max**, opening Fleet Review now checks what your
ships can carry at that moment. Previously, a mine could produce enough while
you were editing that even an immediate launch from Review would ask you to
review the fleet again.

[reporting the failed launches](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1576093300413169745)
and to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[suggesting the fusion reactor check](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1576107895727333397).
