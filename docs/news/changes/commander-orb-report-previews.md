date: '2026-11-18'

Open the commander orb to see where each report happened and how long ago.
Combat headlines distinguish an attack from a defense. Harvests show what your
recyclers collected, and spy contacts identify the sender when available.

Thanks to [Churak](https://discord.com/users/162718073336365056) for
[requesting report ages in the commander view](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1571759322114035752).
