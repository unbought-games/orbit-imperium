---
id: galaxy-stable-flights
title: Galaxy rows stay put when fleets change
date: '2026-11-13'
---

Galaxy now uses compact, even-height rows for occupied and empty positions.
Planet, moon, debris, and fleet information stay together on the left. Select a
planet's image or name, a moon, or a debris field for that target's actions;
player names still open their profiles, and coordinate links keep working.

Fleet chips appear only for active missions and show counts and arrival times
without moving the rows. Selected locations use an outline without adding height.
Galaxy shares the Fleet slots control with Fleets.
Long names truncate while full identities remain available in player profiles.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for reporting
the jumping rows.
