id: galaxy-stable-flights
title: Galaxy rows stay put when fleets change
date: '2026-11-13'
---

Galaxy rows now stay in place when you send probes or fleets arrive. Active
flights replace the planet marker with a compact fleet count and next arrival;
hover, focus, or tap it for each mission's status and exact arrival time.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for reporting
the jumping rows.
