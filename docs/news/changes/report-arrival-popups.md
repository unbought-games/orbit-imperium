title: Quieter report popups
date: '2026-11-18'
---

Archiving reports or returning to a tab after time away now keeps older reports
quiet. A new report can still pop up while the inbox is refreshing.

Thanks to [sumdeos](https://discord.com/users/181133185491402753) for reporting
the issue and recording the archive sequence.
