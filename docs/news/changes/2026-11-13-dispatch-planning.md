Dispatch now shows potential loot beside the cargo controls and tells you whether
your selected ships have room for all of it. The Report link takes you back to the
espionage report. Ship rows include artwork, available counts are plain text,
and Review fleet stays disabled until you select ships.

Preparing an attack in the combat simulator takes you straight to Dispatch's
final review when the fleet passes current checks. You still confirm the launch.
Cargo fill buttons also work while all fleet slots are occupied, so you can
prepare the next flight while waiting for one to return.

Thanks to Churak, Gh0stP1rate, and sumdeos for the reports and suggestions.
