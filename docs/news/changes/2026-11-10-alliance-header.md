The alliance board now opens with a ready-to-write composer. You can publish a
short title, a message, or both. The Markdown reminder sits beneath the writing
area, and Publish stays on the right.

Notices lead with their title and content, with the author, insignia, and date
below. A small menu in the top-right corner holds editing, pinning, and deletion.
Pinned notices stay at the top; each group runs newest first. Loading older
notices extends the feed without replacing the notices already on screen.

The My alliance heading also has balanced spacing and a smaller Discover
alliances button in its usual secondary style.
