title: Report tools and clearer defender lists
date: '2026-11-09T15:00:00Z'
---

The Reports menu and command orb inbox now have **Mark all as read**. It covers
all your reports, including archived reports and pages you haven't loaded, while
keeping stars, shared links, and archive choices intact. **All reports** is at
the top of the orb inbox, so a long unread list can't bury it.

The archive menu remembers your last age preset and custom hours in this browser.
Opening it still lets you review the selection before archiving anything.

If your browser blocks copying a shared report link, we try a second copy method
and select the link for manual copying if that also fails. The message tells you
whether copying succeeded. This improves the fallback for the reported Brave on
iOS problem; it hasn't been verified on an iPhone running Brave.

Combat reports group each defender's ships and defenses separately, including
individual rounds. Participant identities and the Start, Survived, Recovered,
and Lost values stay intact.
