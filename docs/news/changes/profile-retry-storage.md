title: Clearer feedback when a profile edit cannot be sent
---

If your browser cannot save a profile change for safe retry, Orbit now explains
that the change was not sent and keeps your edit on the page. Allow site storage
or free some space, then save again. Error details are available without exposing
your draft or account credentials.
