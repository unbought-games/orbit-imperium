---
id: fleet-dispatch-performance
title: Prepare your next fleet while the first one launches
date: '2026-11-25'
---

Fleet Dispatch prepares launch terms while you edit. Once the server accepts
your order, you can prepare the next fleet. The launch confirmation appears as
soon as the committed result reaches the client. If the order fails, you can
recover it without losing the draft you're working on.

Completed ships and other assets use the universe's current catalog prices for
scoring. Moving ships between a planet and a fleet preserves their value. An
intentional price change in an active universe recalculates current scores.
