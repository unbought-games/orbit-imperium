id: alliance-application-notifications
title: Review alliance applications from the command uplink
date: '2026-11-28T13:00:00Z'
---

When a player applies to your alliance, members who can accept or reject applications get a notification. Open it to review that player's application. The notice leaves the command uplink when the application is handled or withdrawn, or when you lose review access.

Thanks to [Ethan Mick](https://discord.com/users/968967984553033899) for [requesting application notifications](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1575882289957703691).
