title: Your fleet, world by world or empire-wide
date: '2026-11-13'
---

The Fleets page puts stationed ships above traffic, with a switch between the
selected world's inventory and your entire empire. Empire totals include ships
on planets and moons plus flying fleets, including returns and holding fleets.
Queued ships stay separate, so they won't inflate the fleet you already have.
Ship icons and names open the Shipyard. The Away column shows ships launched
from the selected world, including those returning home.

Empire fleet traffic combines your missions and detected incoming fleets in
arrival order. When other players' fleets are detected, use All, Yours or Others
to choose what you see.
