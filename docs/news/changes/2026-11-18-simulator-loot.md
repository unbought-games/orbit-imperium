title: See how much loot your fleet can bring home
date: '2026-11-18'
---

The combat simulator now shows how much you could raid with unlimited cargo
alongside your fleet’s average surviving capacity and average gross haul.
You can also see how many simulated victories brought home the full amount.
Launch fuel is paid separately.

The simulator remembers which sections you open or close on this browser.
When you import an espionage report, you can also use **Review attack** with
**Plan attack** closed. Dispatch still shows the fleet quote before launch.
