title: Consistent planet links and beginner alliance access
date: '2026-11-12T17:00:00Z'

Menu and Rewards now keep your selected planet or moon in the address path, just
like the other game pages. Switching worlds, reloading, and browser history retain
that selection. Older links redirect to the matching address.

Alliance is available under More in beginner mode, so you can reach your alliance
without switching to the full interface.
