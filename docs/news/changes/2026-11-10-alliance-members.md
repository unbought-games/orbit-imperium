title: A compact alliance roster
date: '2026-11-10T15:10:00-05:00'
---

Alliance members now appear in compact rows, with the leader first and the rest
in alphabetical order. Roles, activity, and join dates are easier to scan. On
phones, roles sit beneath each name.

Role assignment and member removal live in each row’s menu. The activity guide
is available beside the column heading, instead of taking up space above the
roster.
