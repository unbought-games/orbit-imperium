id: arrival
title: Arrive at your new world
date: '2026-11-08T18:30:00Z'
---

New empires now start with a short arrival scene instead of a dashboard. You wake
from hypersleep, and the command orb asks how you'd like to be addressed, which of
the worlds suited to your orbit you see out the viewport, and what to call it. Then
it hands you to your Briefing.

Your title, planet look and planet name are ordinary choices: the look shows in
Galaxy for everyone, and nothing here affects gameplay. If you'd rather get straight
to building, Skip keeps what you've picked so far and gives your world a random name.

Existing empires don't see the scene. New players also no longer get feature
announcements for things that shipped before they joined.

This is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Servers apply the new database
records through normal schema preparation; no activation step is needed.
