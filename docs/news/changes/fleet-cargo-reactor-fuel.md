title: Reserve reactor fuel when filling fleet cargo
date: '2026-11-18'
---

When a planet is losing deuterium, **Add all** and **Max deuterium** now leave
enough for one minute of net consumption while you review the fleet. You can
still enter an exact cargo amount yourself.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[reporting the rejected fleet](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1572642319474233474).
