---
id: commander-orb-resume
title: Faster inbox refresh when returning to a tab
date: '2026-11-18'
---

If a probe arrives while your Fleets tab is in the background, the commander orb now checks for unread reports as soon as the tab has a fresh overview. You can open the report from the orb inbox without waiting for its next 15-second check.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for reporting the delay when using espionage to decide whether to recall a fleet.
