---
id: fleet-recall-reports
title: Fewer reports when recalling fleets
date: '2026-11-18'
---

Recalling a fleet no longer adds a “Recalled” report to your inbox. Fleets that
send a report when they arrive home still do.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[suggesting this change](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1571924050781937816).
