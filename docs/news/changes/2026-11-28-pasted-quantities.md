id: pasted-quantities
title: Paste quantities with commas
date: '2026-11-28'
---

You can copy a number like 12,500 from a report and paste it into a quantity
field in the Combat Simulator, Fleet Dispatch, or Shipyard. The field enters
12500 and applies its usual limits.

Thanks to Ethan Mick for reporting this in Discord.
