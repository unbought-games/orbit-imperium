date: '2026-11-15'

Help & community now brings together quick ways to support Orbit Imperium:
vote on BrowserGamesTop, upvote on Foundplay, follow on IndieDB or ModDB,
star the game on GitHub, and follow Unbought Games on GitHub.

These actions are optional and do not award Renown. The page is directly
linkable at `/u/<universe>/settings/support`, using each player’s own planet
context.
