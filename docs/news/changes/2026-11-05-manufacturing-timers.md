---
id: manufacturing-timers
title: Simpler timers for a single production order
date: '2026-11-05T16:41:46Z'
---

When you have one production order left, we now show **Next unit** and
**Until complete** instead of repeating the same order and queue completion time.
This applies in Shipyard, Defense, and Empire Overview.

With multiple orders, you still see **Next unit**, **This order**, and
**Entire queue**. You can tell when your recyclers will finish even if rocket
launchers are queued behind them.

Thanks to [Gh0stP1rate](https://discord.com/users/343178308164255744) for
[reporting the confusing timers](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1567615359312465942).

This change is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your server
depends on its deployment.
