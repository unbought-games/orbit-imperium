title: Compare your defenses
date: '2026-11-09T15:30:00Z'
---

You can now compare defenses from the Defense page or an individual defense’s
details. Select the defenses you’re considering to see their costs, attack,
shield, hull and rapid fire side by side. Switch between base values and your
completed research bonuses. Recovery rules and shield dome limits are included.

Thanks to [Churak](https://discord.com/users/162718073336365056) for
[requesting defense comparison](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1568606040793284630).

This change is included in source builds after 0.6.0. Availability on your server
depends on its deployment.
