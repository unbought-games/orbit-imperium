id: graviton-technology
title: Graviton Technology
date: '2026-11-17'
---

A planet with Research Lab 12 can research Graviton Technology when it produces
300,000 energy. Power already going to your mines counts toward that total.
Once you start, the research takes one second and uses no materials or energy.

Your empire keeps the level even if you lose the satellites that helped you
earn it. The next level takes three times as much power. For now, Graviton is a
research milestone with no additional bonus.
