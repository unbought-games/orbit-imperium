date: '2026-11-18T16:00:00Z'

You can archive a starred report from its detail page and keep the star. If
you've shared the report, Archive asks you to confirm before it stops sharing.
The old link stays unavailable if you move the report back to your inbox.

and [Churak](https://discord.com/channels/1559681008532586586/1560363739818098738/1571148516850663556)
for reporting the problem.
