title: Planet and moon probes in Galaxy and Spy Contact
date: '2026-11-28'
---

Spy Contact reports identify the body the probes came from and the body they reached.
When you spy on both a planet and its moon, Galaxy counts both fleets at that position.
Open the fleet count to see which flight is heading to each body.
