title: Open Galaxy from fleet cards
date: '2026-11-12T21:46:00-05:00'
---

Click or tap a fleet card’s coordinates to open that location in Galaxy.
Right-click or press and hold for location actions. Click the rest of your
fleet’s card to see the mission details.

On the Empire overview, we renamed the fleet movements panel’s label to The
Bridge and moved the fleet status closer to the heading.
