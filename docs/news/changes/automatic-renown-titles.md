id: automatic-renown-titles
title: Renown titles alongside cosmetic choices
date: '2026-11-12T17:30:00Z'
---

Participating servers can award permanent titles automatically at Renown
milestones, alongside the existing cosmetic choice. Rewards shows each title's
name, whether it has been earned, and the separate cosmetic choice available at
that milestone.

Earning a title never changes the title you display. Choose it yourself in your
empire profile whenever you want to use it.
