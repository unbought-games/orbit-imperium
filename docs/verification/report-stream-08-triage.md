The old multi-report glance, desktop range and bulk-read tours remain retired. See the [original report ledger](test-migration-reports.md), [displayed-read verification](report-mark-shown-read.md) and [repair ledger](../runbooks/test-performance-audit.md). The historical report inventory includes a shared readiness change. The touch-edge family also carries the prior disabled-control pointer check. The frozen identity follows the case across migration.

During authoring, the initial component runner reported 10 passes and 2 failures in 13.3s. Both proposed `row corners activate the archive button and protected corners absorb taps` replacements stopped before the first tap. The accessibility snapshot shows two reports with equal observation time. The protected Returned report sorts fourth and the final Delivered report is unprotected. The replacement used the final row for its disabled-button assertion. This was a migration fixture/selector defect. The two failed replacements were retired. The healthy application touch-edge cases remain the evidence for padded and disabled corners. No retry of the failed replacements, increased timeout or production change was used. All final checks and independent review are recorded in the stream handoff.
