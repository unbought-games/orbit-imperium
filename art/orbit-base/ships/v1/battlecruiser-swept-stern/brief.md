## Edit prompt

Art-direct a fresh Orbit Imperium BATTLECRUISER silhouette. Both references are STYLE and shipyard-family references, not hulls to trace. Design one beautiful classic imperial capital ship as a photographed hero miniature, diagonal elevated three-quarter view with the nose lower left, stern upper right. Think of an ocean-going fast battlecruiser's striking sheer line translated into a spaceship: long clean SINGLE chisel bow; pinched, subtly curved waist; a rising armored citadel; dramatic swept-back, broadly flared stern shoulders. Width grows aft. Two clear negative-space notches near the waist. A stepped, faceted shoulder on either side carries a prominent trainable CAPITAL LASER BATTERY aimed slightly outboard and forward, its mount clear of the bow against negative space. Sculpt these as heavy gimballed faceted beam projectors: chunky armored mantlet, short tapered graphite emitter with a bright deep-set ruby red optical aperture and two small unequal-length shielding cheeks. The mount visibly turns into a separate silhouette, distinctly a gun. Use chunky dark hardware and subtle glass reflections instead of tubes, pitchforks, neon beams, railway tracks, parallel rails or enormous glowing headlights. Two off-center weapons; uninterrupted clean bow. A low heavily armored conning tower with tiny grouped bridge panes above a long dark recessed side deck, tiny paired launch doors, fine sparse mechanical details clustered around the weapons; leave bold, generous unbroken plate shapes elsewhere. Small integrated thruster bank at stern with faint green hyperspace interiors. Graceful disciplined surface transitions, classic imperial service craft, a balance of naval weight and aggressive speed. Muted slate-blue primary armor with warm champagne accent plating on selected upper shoulder plates, restrained edge wear, tiny deck lights. Studio lighting and framing consistent with references. Transparent alpha, landscape 3:2, isolate and center the complete ship with margins, no other subjects, no text, no scenery, no beams firing. Make the weapons clear but the whole ship's silhouette the most memorable thing.
