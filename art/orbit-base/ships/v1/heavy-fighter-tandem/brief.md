# Heavy Fighter · tandem assault craft

Game ID `HeavyFighter`. Free Orbit Base design study. Built-in ImageGen edit of the [first-pass Heavy Fighter](../heavy-fighter/master.png) using the [approved Light Fighter](../light-fighter-cannon-pods/master.png) as the family reference. Two seats in tandem, wing-mounted cannon pods, a cropped central bow and recessed armored shoulders are visual interpretations of the owner's brief. The blue impulse drives follow the [Heavy Fighter rules](../../../../../rules/content/heavy-fighter.json).

## Authored color controls

Build the offline [Heavy Fighter package](../../../packages/orbit-base-heavy-fighter/v2/manifest.json) with `npm run art:orbit-base-heavy-fighter`. It uses the same fixed-view finish renderer as the Light Fighter. The [material drawing](livery.svg) covers primary-painted hull and wing plating, accent shoulder fairings and wing tips, and protected canopy, cockpit seats, cannon pods, vents and impulse drives. The [emblem slots](emblem-slots.json) follow the upper wing panels. Revision v2 places the near-wing emblem on the broad panel just aft of the leading-edge seam.

The original reference dyes reconstruct the source pixel for pixel. Independent channel changes were checked on the main wing, shoulder, and wingtip with protection checks on both seats, cannons, engine lights and vents. Pale, dark and saturated diagnostic finishes were inspected at ship size and close up. Both emblem placements were exercised against the authored slots. The feedback exports use emblem-free Red/Ivory, Blue/Ivory, and Blue/Gold recipes from the game's free dye palette. The package is authored for offline review. Hangar registration is separate.

## Edit prompt

Design a revised HEAVY FIGHTER for the Orbit Imperium game. Image 1 is the earlier heavy fighter to revise, image 2 is the approved related light fighter for design-family reference. Deliver ONE single ship alone, elevated front three-quarter view at the same nose-lower-left angle as image 2, fully within canvas. Build a two-crew armored assault fighter recognizable as the broad, stocky big sibling of image 2. Make the crew space a real visual feature: extended, raised smoked tandem canopy with TWO separately recognizable seats and headrests, one behind the other, the rear seat higher, center transverse canopy bow. Let both seats be clearly seen through the glass. Ship's single central nose leads the silhouette: broad short chisel prow forward of the pilot. Retract image 1's huge twin projecting cheek boxes almost entirely back to the cockpit shoulders. Let those form low armored cheek plates integrated into the main hull with champagne upper panels. Extend the wings: a wider span of clean, swept cropped trapezoidal wing panels with thick strong roots and room to see the whole planform. Substantial shoulder and aft fuselage mass, broader centerbody than the light's delicate dart. Two separate prominent dark graphite sci-fi pulse cannon pods under the wings akin to image 2's guns, muscular stepped shrouds and broad silver-rimmed muzzle faces. Pod muzzles extend visibly beyond leading edges against clear space. Make both read unmistakably as guns even at 96px wide. Compact heavy impulse engine pair aft with small BLUE illuminated apertures tucked within the machinery. Heavy plated late-industrial naval miniature, same satin slate-blue primary upper armor and restrained warm champagne secondary shoulders, dark fixed canopy, cannon hardware, blue engines, neutral white key and soft fill. Transparent background, 3:2 horizontal 1536x1024, no other ship, no text, no labels, no insignias, no firing, no plumes, no background shadow. Not a copy of the Light Fighter: broader armored proportions, span, two-seat cockpit, chisel bow and boxy blue engines are distinctive.
