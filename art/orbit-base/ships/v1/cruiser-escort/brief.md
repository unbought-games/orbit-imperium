# Cruiser · crewed escort study

Game ID `Cruiser`. Revised from the [first Cruiser](../cruiser/master.png), with the [Battlecruiser](../battlecruiser/master.png) as a family reference. The [game rules](../../../../../rules/content/cruiser.json) give the Cruiser impulse propulsion, an Ion Technology prerequisite and rapid fire against Light Fighters and Rocket Launchers. The two deck turrets, forward gun deck, bridge windows, hatch and crew ports are visual interpretations.

The [family comparison](family-comparison.webp) depicts the Cruiser smaller to explore the relationship. Relative lengths and crew complement are art-direction choices under review. The transparent master was edited with built-in ImageGen. The draft paint mask and [96 px check](thumbnail-check.png) support further study. Free Orbit Base art under the repository artwork license.

## Edit prompt

Precise art-direction revision of image 1, the Orbit Imperium Cruiser. Image 2 is its larger Battlecruiser sibling for design language only. Deliver exactly ONE Cruiser on transparent alpha. Retain image 1's recognizable swallowtail stern, twin outboard swept armored shoulders, small blue impulse engine aperture, slender centerline, clean voids, slate-blue primary hull armor and champagne panel finish. Same maintained physical miniature, neutral studio light, elevated front three-quarter camera, nose lower left. Keep the pointed bow but pull in its longest empty point by about a fifth and give it a slightly fuller forecastle. Three specific changes: 1) Put TWO visibly separate medium sci-fi ion gun turrets on the forward dorsal deck in line ahead of the bridge, second slightly raised. Each a low faceted rotating mount with a stout projecting graphite barrel with a distinct dark muzzle. Both should be readable as naval guns at thumbnail size; modest relative to the ship, no enormous siege ordnance or firing effects. Keep their silhouettes cleanly separated from each other in perspective. 2) Make the aft bridge a compact enclosed multi-person wheelhouse with a narrow band of several individual dark glazed panes and a single short communications mast. Below it add a short staggered row of small illuminated wardroom ports on the near flank and one neatly recessed crew access hatch with a tiny frame as a human-scale reference. No fighter canopy, seats visible from space, crowds, neon stripes, rows of a hundred windows, or chaotic greebles. 3) Preserve the family resemblance through the sharp keel, clipped armor edges and champagne shoulder lines; keep its stern broader and the foredeck shorter than image 2's long parallel blade form. The new foredeck guns and inhabited bridge should catch the eye before the surface detail. Primary paint and accent follow removable panels, fixed glass and weapons and engine metal stay their own materials. No insignia, no lettering, UI, planets, stars, labels, ground or cast shadow. 3:2 landscape catalog master, full centered cutout all extremities inside the frame with a little margin.
