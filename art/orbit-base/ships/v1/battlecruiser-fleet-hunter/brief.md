Revise image 1: the Orbit Base BATTLECRUISER, an imperial capital-ship hunter. Image 2 is the smaller Cruiser only for the shipyard's visual vocabulary and physical size of crew-scale details. Output a single isolated Battlecruiser. Preserve image 1's memorable silhouette: extended knife prow flanked by two enormous forward-facing forked laser lances. Keep the actual forked muzzle, recessed icy blue rectangular lens, and clear gap between jaws; retain a steel tip on the central blade. Make the whole ship convincingly MUCH LARGER, dangerous and built around its spinal lasers. Develop the midbody aft of the emitters into a long, deep armored naval hull. Show the lances' long segmented graphite breeches between a few broad champagne armored collars integrated into the side blades. A single visually subordinate secondary battery of three small repeated recessed laser casemates on the visible flank: dark shallow recess, short angled emitter head with a pinprick of icy light; broad spacing and discipline, all smaller than the main battery. A compact low stepped command island well back on the centerline with two shallow tiers of tiny divided bridge panes, one unobtrusive sensor array. Along the tall near-facing hull side: short interrupted runs of very small dim warm crew ports on successive decks, two small pressure hatches and a recessed boat bay with a closed segmented door for human scale. Relative to hull size make window modules much smaller than on Cruiser reference. Maintain long unbroken armor planes forward and two dark open channels between the needle and lances so the graphic outline is clear at 96px. Strong military presence at idle; substantial and fast, weight and power through form.
